# Seasonal landscapes

Created using the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-10-30. Each image was generated independently from the common prompt followed by its seasonal suffix, with `transparent_background: false`. No source image or external image service is required at runtime. The three final PNGs are saved alongside this document.

## Common prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final landscape background artwork for the Bulgarian Patilanci Minecraft launcher, used both as a wide hero banner and a full loading screen. Create an exceptionally polished cinematic Minecraft voxel environment, high quality shader lighting, detailed block architecture and natural terrain, not a UI mockup. Landscape 16:9 composition. Bulgarian mountain village with red-tile roofs, timber and white plaster houses and stone arches, subtle Bulgarian white green red flag on tower. Keep left third quieter and darker for overlaid interface copy; most atmospheric landmarks on center and right; focal features in central horizontal 45% band to survive wide banner cropping. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no characters close to camera. All seasonal details must be physically integrated into the voxel environment, not flat stickers or vector shapes.
