Prompt: Create a polished raster background asset for a Minecraft modpack launcher hero card, cinematic voxel game screenshot aesthetic. Wide landscape 1536x864. A Minecraft Warden and a blocky adventurer on the right half in an ancient underground sculk city, oxidized teal and cyan glowing details, dark slate stone, subtle warm amber lighting upper left, black-green shadows. Recognizably Minecraft cubic geometry and pixelated textures, beautiful moody in-game shader lighting, crisp voxel geometry, not photorealistic. Composition: subject large on right side, left 50 percent mostly dark atmospheric cave and low detail for white text to overlay. Rich deep greens and teal, no purple cast. Full bleed background only, no letters, text, interface, buttons, watermark or border.
